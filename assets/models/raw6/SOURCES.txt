  Modifications (blender/shark.py):
    - removed a stray helper mesh (42-vertex icosphere), Sketchfab root empties and the 32 s root-motion "Circling" clip;
    - baked all node transforms into the armature and mesh; scaled from 16.9 units to 4.5 m long; centred at the origin;
    - welded duplicate vertices; mouth-interior (palate/throat) geometry decimated to 30 %, body kept at full resolution
      (51.2k -> 32.0k triangles); unused extra UV sets removed; smooth normals;
    - KHR_materials_pbrSpecularGlossiness material converted to metallic-roughness: metallic 0, roughness 0.5-0.8
      (mean 0.56) derived from the gloss map, ambient occlusion packed into the ORM texture (R), base colour and normal map unchanged;
    - "Swimming" clip renamed "Swim", retimed from 1.97 s to 1.583 s (38 frames at 24 fps) with an exact loop;
      "Biting" clip renamed "Bite" (1.0 s, includes a short forward lunge of the root bone).
