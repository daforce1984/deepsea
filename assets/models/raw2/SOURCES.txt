raw2 model sources (compiled 2026-09-26). All files: glTF 2.0 GLB, PNG/JPEG textures (1024 px), no Draco/KTX2.
Coordinates: metres, +Y up, head/front faces +Z (glTF). Left side of the animal = +X.
Downloaded from the Objaverse mirror of Sketchfab CC-licensed models (https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/objaverse);
the licence was checked in each file's embedded Sketchfab asset metadata.
CC-BY 4.0 requires attribution in credits plus a note that the files were modified.

crab.glb  - Snow crab (Chionoecetes opilio), photogrammetry scan
  Author: ffish.asia / floraZia.com (https://sketchfab.com/ffishAsia-and-floraZia)
  Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/snow-crab-chionoecetes-opilio-959f91a4aa9549e29d3f69019b9df593
  Licence: CC-BY 4.0. Changes: colour-checker cube removed, decimated from 622k to 36k tris, unlit material changed to lit PBR,
  scaled, re-posed (crouched), auto-rigged (39 bones), procedural Walk/Idle animations added.
