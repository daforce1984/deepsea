manta.glb - Giant oceanic manta ray (Mobula birostris; hand-sculpted giant-manta anatomy: rolled/forward cephalic fins,
  terminal mouth, eyes on the sides of the head, five pairs of ventral gill slits, small dorsal fin, pelvic fins, whip
  tail). Recoloured to the oceanic-manta pattern: black back with two white shoulder patches, white belly with dark spots
  concentrated on the abdomen, grey ventral wing margins. Replacement for raw/manta_ray.glb (Quaternius CC0 low-poly).
  4.5 m wingspan (rest pose), 4.30 m long incl. tail, 37,430 tris, 22,011 vertices, one mesh / one material.
  Rigged: 16 joints (root, spine_front, spine_rear, tail1-3, ceph_L/R, wing1-4_L/R), <= 4 weights per vertex.
  Animation "Swim": exact loop, 97 samples, 0.0-4.0 s (0.25 Hz at speed 1; first and last keys identical), symmetric wing
  beat with a travelling wave toward the tips, chord twist, slight body heave/pitch, trailing tail wave, cephalic-fin flutter.
  The game's loader config P('manta_ray', { len: 4.5, fwd: '+z', anim: 'Swim', lenAxis: 0 }) fits this file unchanged
  (frame-0 span = 4.47 m, i.e. 0.7 % under the rest span, so the game's frame-0 normalisation is within 1 %).

  Modifications (blender/manta.py):
    - removed the source's 31-joint FBX rig and its clip (3.6 s, ~0.6 Hz, not an exact loop); kept the bind-pose mesh;
    - baked all transforms; scaled from 241.5 source units to a 4.5 m wingspan; head -> +Z (source head was at glTF -Z;
      verified from the geometry: the +Z end is the two-lobed cephalic-fin end, 0.69 m wide; the -Z end is the 1.7 cm
      tail tip); dorsal -> +Y (verified: up-facing texels are the dark side); centred; welded coincident shell vertices;
    - recoloured the saturated navy-blue / lavender paint to natural tones (luminance-mapped: black back, white belly);
      darkened the painted light-blue sheen streaks and the pale crown on the back, keeping only the white shoulder patches;
    - decimated symmetrically 88,624 -> 37,430 tris; new UV layout (smart UV project); smooth normals;
    - baked from the full-resolution source (Cycles CPU): base colour 2048 px; tangent-space normal map 1024 px (source
      geometry + a fine procedural skin grain); ambient occlusion into ORM.R; roughness 0.57-0.70 (mean 0.63; black back
      a little rougher than the belly) in ORM.G; metallic 0; double-sided (top and bottom are open single-sheet shells);
    - new 16-joint rig with smooth geometric weights (spanwise blend functions along each 4-bone wing chain, no wing
      influence on the head; body split between front/rear spine, tail chain and cephalic fins);
    - new exact-loop "Swim" clip (96 frames at 24 fps).
