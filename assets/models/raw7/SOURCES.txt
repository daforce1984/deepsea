deepfish.glb - Grenadier / rattail (Coryphaenoides marginatus, Macrouridae), photogrammetry scan of a real specimen.
  Stand-in for the abyssal grenadier (Coryphaenoides armatus, same genus, the commonest large fish seen on the
  abyssal floor, down to ~5000 m, up to ~1 m long); scaled to 1.0 m total length.
  Title:   ヘリダラ Amami Grenadier, Coryphaenoides marginatus
  Author:  ffish.asia / floraZia.com (https://sketchfab.com/ffishAsia-and-floraZia)
  Source:  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/amami-grenadier-coryphaenoides-marginatus-608ceff39222456fbbcd9f0237e67ac7
           (Objaverse uid 608ceff39222456fbbcd9f0237e67ac7, glbs/000-081)
  Licence: CC-BY 4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
  Credit line: "Amami Grenadier, Coryphaenoides marginatus" by ffish.asia / floraZia.com, CC-BY 4.0, modified.
  Modifications (blender/deepfish.py):
    - removed the colour-checker cube; joined the 6 scan chunks, welded their seams, removed 96 floating scan fragments;
    - baked all transforms; scaled from 19.2 units to 1.0 m long; head -> +Z, dorsal -> +Y; centred at the origin;
      removed the specimen's slight sideways bend (cubic centre-line fit, max 3.8 cm);
    - decimated 442.7k -> 35.6k triangles; new UV layout; smooth normals;
    - unlit scan texture (1024 px) re-baked onto the new UVs at 2048 px; tangent-space normal map (1024 px) baked
      from the full-resolution scan (normals on the thin fins clamped to <= 70 deg to remove back-face bake errors);
      ambient occlusion baked into ORM.R;
    - metallic-roughness material: metallic 0, roughness 0.52-0.72 (mean 0.62) derived from texture luminance,
      double-sided (fins are single-sheet surfaces). No rig/animation (the game swims static meshes procedurally along +Z).
