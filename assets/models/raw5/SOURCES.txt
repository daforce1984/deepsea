humpback.glb - Humpback whale (Megaptera novaeangliae), sculpted and retopologised, rigged and animated for this project
  Title: "Game-ready Humpback Whale"   Author: Allie2k (https://sketchfab.com/Allie2k)
  Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/game-ready-humpback-whale-da07e3ff73914ff28d2b8cf9da794036
          (Objaverse uid da07e3ff73914ff28d2b8cf9da794036)
  Licence: CC-BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
  Changes: loose mesh pieces welded (UV seams kept); one Catmull-Clark subdivision level (4,968 -> 29,808 tris);
  scaled to 14.0 m long; pectoral fins lengthened along their axis from 3.7 m to 4.5 m (about 1/3 of body length);
  centred; material set to metallic 0 / roughness 0.55 (original base colour and normal maps kept unchanged);
  12-bone armature added (Root, Chest, Head, Spine1-3, Tail, Flukes, Fin1/Fin2 .L/.R) with procedural smooth
  weights, and a 7.0 s looping "Swim" clip (dorso-ventral travelling wave with fluke up/down stroke,
  gentle pectoral flap and twist). Build script: blender/humpback.py.
