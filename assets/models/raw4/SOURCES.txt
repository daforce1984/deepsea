raw4 model sources (compiled 2026-09-26). All files: glTF 2.0 GLB, embedded 1024 px PNG/JPEG textures, metallic-roughness PBR,
no Draco/KTX2/extensions. Units: metres. Up: +Y. Head: +Z. Centred at the origin (bbox centre). Left side of the animal = +X.
Downloaded from the Objaverse mirror of Sketchfab CC-licensed models (https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/objaverse).
Licence checked in both the Objaverse annotation and the file's embedded Sketchfab metadata (asset.extras.license).
CC-BY 4.0 requires attribution in the credits and a note that the files were modified.
