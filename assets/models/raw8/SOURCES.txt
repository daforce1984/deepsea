squid.glb - Giant squid (Architeuthis dux) stand-in, built from a photogrammetry scan of a real Japanese flying squid
  (Todarodes pacificus, Ommastrephidae). No realistic CC-BY/CC0 Architeuthis exists in Objaverse 1.0 (see rejected list), so the
  closest realistic squid scan (same torpedo mantle / terminal fins / 8 arms + 2 tentacles body plan) was re-proportioned to a
  giant squid: mantle 2.0 m, arms ~3.0 m, total length 10.0 m including the feeding tentacles.
  Title:   スルメイカ Japanese Flying Squid, Todarodes pacificus
  Author:  ffish.asia / floraZia.com (https://sketchfab.com/ffishAsia-and-floraZia)
  Source:  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/japanese-flying-squid-todarodes-pacificus-5f0688cb4ab64a398784118130987a15
           (Objaverse uid 5f0688cb4ab64a398784118130987a15, glbs/000-033)
  Licence: CC-BY 4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
  Credit line: "Japanese Flying Squid, Todarodes pacificus" by ffish.asia / floraZia.com, CC-BY 4.0, modified.
  Modifications (blender/squid.py):
    - removed the colour-checker cube; joined the 22 scan chunks, welded their seams (no floating debris found);
    - unfolded the two feeding tentacles (scanned folded back in a U along the head and mantle): a centre line was fitted through
      each tentacle and every vertex past the U-bend re-embedded along a line trailing behind the arms (cross-sections preserved);
      tentacle stalks lengthened ~3.2-3.6x, clubs ~1.4x;
    - arm crown stretched 2.57x along the body axis (arms ~3 m); fins narrowed laterally to 70 % (Architeuthis has small fins);
      eyes enlarged 2x with a smooth radial falloff (dark eye ~16 cm across, eyeball region ~25 cm, as in Architeuthis);
    - baked all transforms; scaled to mantle 2.0 m / total 10.0 m; mantle tip -> +Z, dorsal -> +Y; centred at the origin;
    - decimated 1.51M -> 47.5k triangles; new UV layout (smart project on a smoothed copy, concave island packing); smooth normals;
    - unlit scan textures (2 x 1024 px) re-baked onto the new UVs at 2048 px; tangent-space normal map (1024 px) baked from the
      full-resolution deformed scan (normals clamped to <= 70 deg on the thin fins/arm membranes); ambient occlusion baked into ORM.R;
    - metallic-roughness material: metallic 0, roughness 0.35-0.48 (mean 0.40, wet skin) derived from texture luminance,
      double-sided (fins are single-sheet surfaces). No rig/animation (the game swims static meshes procedurally along +Z).
  Landmarks (glTF coords, metres): mantle tip z = +5.01, mantle opening z ~ +3.0, eyes ~(+0.21, 0.04, 2.17) and (-0.12, 0.05, 2.18),
    arm bases z ~ +1.9, arm tips z ~ -1.1, tentacle club tips z = -5.01. Bbox 0.94 x 0.50 x 10.02 m.
  Colour: the specimen is a fresh-dead squid (reddish-brown dorsal mantle, pale head/arms; chromatophores partly relaxed).
